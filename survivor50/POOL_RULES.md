# Survivor 50 Pool Rules

## How It Works

- **Each week**, before the episode airs, you vote for one player still in the game.
- **If that player is voted out, medically evacuated, or leaves the game that episode, you're eliminated from the pool.**
- You **can only vote for each player once** across the entire season. Once you've used a name, it's burned.

## Strategy

The goal is to survive the full season without picking someone who goes home the week you pick them.

- **Safe picks**: Vote for players who seem safe *this* week but might be in danger *soon*. You burn their name either way, so ideally you use a player's name right before they become a target — that way you get value from their "safe" week and they can't be wasted later when they're actually in danger.
- **Dangerous picks**: Voting for someone currently on the bottom, in a minority alliance, or whose tribe just lost is risky — they could go home that night.
- **Wasted picks**: Voting for someone who's going to coast safely for many weeks "wastes" their name. You could have saved that pick for a week when you had fewer safe options.

## Ideal Play

The dream scenario:
1. You vote for Player X in Week N.
2. Player X survives Week N (you're safe).
3. Player X gets voted out in Week N+1 or soon after (their name is already burned anyway, so no loss).

This means you want to pick players who are **safe this week** but **on borrowed time**.
