A working definition

What counts as
AI-FI?

Fiction where computation changes the shape of being: artificial minds, uploaded people, simulated worlds, posthuman evolution, and civilizations rewritten by technology.

This leaves the AI industry nonfiction on the other shelf. Space opera makes the cut when its ideas about intelligence, identity, or technology are doing real work.
